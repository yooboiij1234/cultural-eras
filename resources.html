Project focus:


The goal of this project is to examine how technology and media have influenced music,fashion, entertainment, communication, and popular culture from 1998-2026.

Conclusion:


The cultural changes of the past several decades demonstrate how closely technology and popular culture are actually connected. As new technologies develop, they continue to influence how people communicate, create, share information, and experience entertainment.
