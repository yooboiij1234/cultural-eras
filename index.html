Welcome:



Culture is constantly changing as technology, entertainment, fashion, music and social media influence the way people communicate and experience the world. This project explores major cultural changes from 1998 through 2026.

About this project:



From the late 1990's to the present, cultural trends have changed dramatically. The rise of digital technology, social media, streaming platforms, smartphones, and artificial intelligence has transformed how people consume entertainment and connect with one another. This website explores some of the major cultural developments that shaped each era.

What You'll Explore:

Music- Explore how music changed from CDs and MP3 players to streaming platforms and digital media.

Technology- Follow the transition from dial-up internet and desktop computers to smartphones and artificial intelligence.

Fashion- Examine how clothing and style trends changed from the late 1990s to today.

Entertainment- Explore the transition from VHS, DVDs, and cable television to streaming and on-demand entertainment.

Social Media- Examine how online communication developed from early social networks into today's social-media culture.

Explore the Cultural Eras:

1998-2004: The Digital Beginning

2005-2011: The Rise of Social Media

2012-2019: The Smartphone and Streaming Era

2020-2026: The AI and Digital Culture Era
